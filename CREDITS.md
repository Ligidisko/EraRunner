# Credits

Era Runner is a free, non-commercial hackathon game. Every asset is original or openly licensed.

## Music

| Era | Track | Author | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1985 (arcade shooter) | "Dino" (instrumental) | Kim Lightyear (KLY) | CC-BY 4.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/dino |
| 1995 (platformer) | "Litany to the Datastream Goddess" v1.11 | FoxSynergy | CC-BY 3.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/datastream-goddess |
| 2005 (rhythm game) | "Menu Music" (awesomeness.wav) | mrpoly | CC0 | https://opengameart.org/content/menu-music |

The tracks are used unmodified, except that they are sped up during boss fights and power-ups.
The 2005 rhythm chart is generated automatically from the "Menu Music" audio at load time.

## Everything else

| Asset | Author | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprites (player, aliens, bosses, memory items, backgrounds) | Era Runner team, drawn in code | Original | `src/gfx/pixelArt.js`, `src/eras/*.js` |
| Sound effects and fallback music | Era Runner team, synthesized in code (Web Audio) | Original | `src/audio/audio.js` |
| Press Start 2P font | CodeMan38 | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Press+Start+2P |
| Phaser 3 game framework | Phaser Studio | MIT | https://phaser.io |

When adding a CC0 or CC-BY asset (Kenney.nl, OpenGameArt), add a row here **and** to the itch.io page:
title, author, license, link. CC-BY assets require this credit; CC0 doesn't, but we credit anyway.

All pop-culture references are generic objects from each decade (cassettes, floppy disks, flip phones).
No trademarked characters, logos, or copyrighted songs are used.
